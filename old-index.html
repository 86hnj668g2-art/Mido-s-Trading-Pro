NEWS ANALYSIS

USD + MAJOR GLOBAL EVENTS

Economic news intelligence for XAU/USD gold traders.

NFP – Non-Farm Payrolls

HIGH IMPACT

Measures US employment growth. Stronger-than-expected results can support USD and pressure gold. Weaker results can have the opposite effect, depending on market expectations.

CPI – US Inflation

HIGH IMPACT

Inflation data influences Federal Reserve interest-rate expectations. Higher-than-expected inflation can support USD and weigh on gold.

FOMC – Federal Reserve

HIGH IMPACT

Interest-rate decisions, policy statements and Fed speeches can cause major movements in gold and the US dollar.

PPI – Producer Price Index

MEDIUM / HIGH IMPACT

Measures producer inflation and can influence expectations for future consumer prices.

GDP – US Economic Growth

HIGH IMPACT

Economic growth figures can influence the dollar, bond yields and gold sentiment.

Unemployment Claims

MEDIUM IMPACT

Weekly labour-market data that can influence USD expectations.

GLOBAL GEOPOLITICAL EVENTS

VARIABLE / HIGH IMPACT

Wars, international tensions, trade disputes and political instability can increase demand for safe-haven assets such as gold.

GLOBAL CENTRAL BANK DECISIONS

HIGH IMPACT

Major central-bank policy decisions can affect currencies, interest rates and international gold demand.
