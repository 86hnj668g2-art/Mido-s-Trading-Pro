Analysis checklist

Review RSI (14), EMA 20, EMA 50 and EMA 200. Check trend direction, support and resistance, and candle confirmation before considering a trade.

Trading Signal Guide

BUY consideration

Price above EMA 20 and EMA 50, bullish momentum, RSI supporting the move and confirmation at support.

SELL consideration

Price below EMA 20 and EMA 50, bearish momentum, RSI supporting the move and confirmation at resistance.

WAIT

Mixed indicators, sideways market, major news approaching or no clear entry confirmation.

These are educational conditions, not live calculated signals. Always verify indicators on the chart.
